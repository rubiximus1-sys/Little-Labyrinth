Privacy Policy

Little Labyrinth
Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Who we are

Rubiximus (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides Little Labyrinth, an Android puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, view advertisements or contact us.

Privacy contact: rubiximus1@gmail.com.

2. Game progress and information stored on your device

Little Labyrinth does not require you to create a game account. The game stores information on your device to support gameplay, including:

  • Completed and unlocked levels, stars, scores, move and push records, collected gems and challenge progress.
  • Saved move sequences used for solution review and local play statistics, such as attempts, restarts and time spent.
  • Control preferences, sound and vibration settings, and cosmetic selections.
  • Insight Token balance and important-hint unlocks. The current version does not ask for your age or exact birth date. If you selected an age group in an earlier version, that local preference may remain on your device; an existing under-13 selection is used to restrict fullscreen ads. We do not upload that saved selection to a server operated by us.

The game’s local-save system does not send these saved progress records to a server operated by us. Advertising services described below can separately collect interaction and diagnostic information. Your device or operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device settings.

3. Advertising and information handled by Google AdMob

The game uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads may include optional rewarded ads for extra Insight Tokens, interstitial ads during selected restart or next-level transitions, and banner ads during transitions. Fullscreen ads are disabled if this installation retains an under-13 selection from an earlier version.

Google and parties involved in serving ads may collect and process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location: an IP address may be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device or other identifiers: the advertising SDK may process app set IDs and other identifiers described in Google’s SDK disclosures, subject to the child-directed configuration. The game’s Android build configuration removes the advertising ID (AD_ID) permission to prevent access to the Android advertising ID through that permission.
  • App and advertising interactions: for example, app launches, taps, ad impressions, video views and other interactions.
  • Diagnostics: information about app and SDK performance, such as launch time, hangs and energy usage.
  • Privacy preferences: consent choices and related signals used to determine which advertising processing is permitted.

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, support advertising analytics, prevent fraud and invalid traffic, and maintain the security and reliability of advertising services. The game is intended for teens and adults and does not verify a player’s age or assume that every player is an adult. Ad requests use a maximum content rating of G (General Audience), non-personalized advertising, and restrictive age-treatment settings. Because exact ages are unknown, under-age-of-consent protections are applied conservatively to all players through the advertising and consent SDKs. These may result in stricter treatment than the teen setting alone. Interest-based advertising and remarketing are disabled through this configuration. Ads that are not personalized can still require information for delivery, measurement, frequency controls or fraud prevention.

Google explains its practices in its Privacy Policy, information about partner sites and apps, and Google Mobile Ads Unity data disclosure.

4. Information you send when contacting us

If you contact us, we receive the email address or other contact details you use and the information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem or screenshots. We use it to respond, provide support and address privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, financial details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

5. How information is used and shared

We use locally stored information to save progress, apply your preferences and provide game features. We use support correspondence to answer your requests and maintain the game. Advertising information is processed by Google and relevant advertising service participants for the purposes described above, under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We do not upload the game’s local progress database to advertising providers through a custom game-progress integration. This does not prevent the advertising SDK from collecting its own interactions and diagnostics. Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or protect users, the game and our rights.

6. Your choices

  • Rewarded ads: eligible players can choose whether to watch an ad for extra Insight Tokens. Declining or not completing it does not grant that reward. Every age group receives two free hint tiers and two Undos per attempt. Important hints cost Insight Tokens, which everyone can earn by completing or replaying levels without ads. An important-hint unlock remains available for that level after restarting.
  • Consent choices: where a consent message is presented, you can make choices through that message. When available or required, open the game’s Settings and select Ad privacy choices to review applicable choices.
  • Device advertising controls: your Android settings may let you manage advertising privacy, including resetting or deleting your advertising ID. Availability and menu names depend on your device.
  • Local data: you can clear the game’s storage through Android app settings or uninstall the game. Doing so can erase your progress and preferences. Device backups may restore data separately.

Changing advertising preferences or deleting an advertising ID does not necessarily stop all advertising data processing. For information about Google-held information and available controls, consult Google My Ad Center and Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed, reset or replaced, subject to device backup behavior. We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain relevant records or meet applicable legal obligations.

Google and other advertising service participants determine retention of information they process under their own policies and applicable requirements. Clearing local game data does not automatically delete information already processed by an advertising provider. Contact us about information you sent directly to us; use Google’s privacy resources for requests concerning information held by Google.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle. Google states that information collected through the Google Mobile Ads Unity plugin is encrypted in transit using TLS. Local game saves rely on your device’s storage protections. No system provides absolute security.

Advertising and support service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe relevant processing arrangements and safeguards.

9. Children’s privacy

Little Labyrinth is a general puzzle game designed and marketed for teens aged 13 and older and adults; it is not specifically directed to children under 13. A store content rating describes content suitability and does not change this intended audience. The game does not require an account, name or email address to play. Local game progress is stored as described above.

The game does not ask players to select an age group. Advertising uses G-rated content filtering, non-personalized requests and conservative under-age-of-consent treatment for all players. An under-13 selection retained from an older version disables rewarded and interstitial ads on that installation. These settings restrict ad targeting and eligible content; they do not mean advertising services collect no information. Advertising services may process information as described in Section 3. Parents and guardians can review the game’s advertising privacy choices, where available, and the relevant device privacy settings. A request concerning information sent directly to Rubiximus can be made through the contact address below.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the privacy contact above so we can review the concern and take appropriate action.
